Our Story

Bangkok's street markets, right by UCF

Lert Thai opened in 2026 on Collegiate Way, bringing the bold flavors of Thailand's street markets to East Orlando. Every dish is made fresh daily using traditional recipes — crispy pork belly noodles, BBQ pork rice, garlic-lime pork salad and rich noodle soups, all built on authentic Thai herbs and spices.

  • ✓

    Cooked fresh daily — traditional recipes, real Thai herbs, no shortcuts.
